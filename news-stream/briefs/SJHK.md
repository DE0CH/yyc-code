# Secretary for Justice Paul Lam Ting-kwok (林定國), his deputy and the Department of Justice

Status 5 November 2026.

## What it is
The Secretary for Justice (律政司司長, SJ) is one of the three Secretaries of Department of the HKSAR government, after the Chief Secretary and the Financial Secretary; nominated by the Chief Executive, appointed by the State Council. He heads the Department of Justice (律政司, DoJ), the government's law office: it controls criminal prosecutions "free from any interference" (Basic Law Article 63), drafts every government bill and advises the government on law. The SJ sits on ExCo, chairs the Law Reform Commission and is a member of the Committee for Safeguarding National Security (國家安全委員會). Paul Lam has held the post since 1 July 2022. Deputy Secretary for Justice (律政司副司長, DSJ) since the same day: Cheung Kwok-kwan 張國鈞 (Horace Cheung, ex-DAB legislator).

## Names
林定國 / 林定国, Paul Lam, Paul TK Lam, Mr Paul Lam SC, 律政司長, 林司長, SJ (also his car plate), GBS SC JP. Recurring remarks: the US as 胖虎 (Gian) and Hong Kong as not 大雄 (Nobita); 「被激到生蝦咁跳」 (2025, on sanctions).

## Background
Born 1968, Wah Yan College, HKU and Nottingham; barrister since 1992, Senior Counsel 2013, head of Parkside Chambers; Bar Association chairman 2017–18; Consumer Council chairman; deputy High Court judge. Fifth SJ after Elsie Leung 梁愛詩, Wong Yan-lung 黃仁龍, Rimsky Yuen 袁國強 and Teresa Cheng 鄭若驊. Acted as Chief Executive on 23 April 2025.

## The Department of Justice
Justice Place (律政中心), Central. Divisions: Prosecutions (刑事檢控科; Director of Public Prosecutions 刑事檢控專員 Anthony Chau 周天行 since 21 May 2026, after Maggie Yang 楊美琪), National Security Prosecutions (維護國家安全檢控科), Civil (民事法律科), Constitutional and Policy Affairs (Solicitor General 法律政策專員), International Law (國際法律科), Law Drafting (法律草擬科); LEAD Office (法治建設辦公室); Hong Kong International Legal Talents Training Academy. Annual Hong Kong Legal Week (香港法律周).

## National security cases
Under NSL Article 46 Lam ordered no-jury trials (three designated judges) for the 47 primary-election case (47人案, 初選案), Jimmy Lai 黎智英 / Apple Daily and the Hong Kong Alliance 支聯會. Lai: convicted December 2025, 20 years on 9 February 2026, no appeal. 47 case: 12 appeals and DoJ's appeal on Lawrence Lau 劉偉聰 dismissed 23 February 2026. Alliance: Lee Cheuk-yan 李卓人 7 years, Chow Hang-tung 鄒幸彤 7 years 3 months, Albert Ho 何俊仁 (September 2026). Joshua Wong 黃之鋒: 10 years for collusion on 5 November 2026. Earlier: the fight over British counsel for Lai and the NPCSC interpretation (2022–23), the 「願榮光歸香港」 injunction (granted on appeal May 2024), Article 23 law (2024), 2026 amendments to the Article 43 rules (device passwords). Vocabulary: 軟對抗 ("soft resistance"), 開放型安全 ("open security"), 勾結外國勢力, 煽惑顛覆, 煽動刊物.

- May 2026: online allegations against new DPP Anthony Chau; DoJ called them smears and referred them to police; Lam's memo asked staff to report their source.

- After the Wang Fuk Court (宏福苑) fire report (29 October 2026): Lam announced laws making bid-rigging (圍標) in building maintenance a crime, up to 10 years and HK$10 million.
- Legal hub: IOMed (國際調解院, HQ in Hong Kong, Teresa Cheng secretary-general); UNIDROIT Asia-Pacific Liaison Office opened 2 November 2026 at Legal Week 2026 (2–6 November); Arbitration Ordinance amendments; trips to Hainan and Brunei (October 2026).

## Easily confused
律政司 = the department since 1997 (before, the Attorney General himself); the US Department of Justice; the Judiciary (司法機構: judges decide, DoJ prosecutes); the Security Bureau (鄧炳強) and national security police, who investigate; China's Ministry of Justice (司法部) and procuratorates (檢察院); Paul Chan 陳茂波 (Financial Secretary) and Carrie Lam 林鄭月娥; IOMed vs the International Organization for Migration.
