# Financial Secretary Paul Chan Mo-po (陳茂波), his deputy, his office and the Budget

Status 22 October 2026.

## What it is
The Financial Secretary (財政司司長, FS) is one of the three Secretaries of Department of the HKSAR government. He oversees financial, monetary, economic, trade, development, housing, transport, innovation and industry policy, controls the Exchange Fund (外匯基金) with the HKMA, sits on ExCo, and delivers the annual Budget (財政預算案) and its Appropriation Bill (撥款條例草案) to LegCo. Paul Chan Mo-po has held the post since 16 January 2017. Deputy Financial Secretary (財政司副司長) since July 2022: Michael Wong Wai-lun 黃偉綸, who acts as FS (署理財政司司長) when Chan is abroad.

## Names
陳茂波 / 陈茂波, 財爺, 財政司長, 波叔, 茂波, 整餅阿茂, older forum nicknames 劏房波 and 囤地波, "finance chief", FS, Paul MP Chan. His weekly blog 司長隨筆. Office: Financial Secretary's Office (財政司司長辦公室, FSO).

## Background
Born 1955, accountant (ex-HKICPA president), LegCo accountancy member 2008–12, Secretary for Development 2012–17 (subdivided-flat 劏房 and Kwu Tung farmland 古洞 controversies; a libel case; a drink-driving report), FS under Leung Chun-ying, Carrie Lam and John Lee.

## Under him
Bureaux: Financial Services and the Treasury (許正宇 Christopher Hui), Commerce and Economic Development (丘應樺), Innovation, Technology and Industry (孫東), Development (甯漢豪), Housing (何永賢), Transport and Logistics (陳美寶); HKMA (Eddie Yue 余偉文), SFC (Julia Leung 梁鳳儀); Government Economist; OASES (引進重點企業辦公室, strategic enterprises, 142 signed by October 2026); he chairs the HKIC board and the Committee on AI+ and Industry Development Strategy.

## Budget and public finance
- Deficits after the pandemic; 2025-26 Budget: pay freeze, spending cuts, about 10,000 civil-service posts deleted. 2025-26 ended in surplus (HK$11.2 billion after bond flows; critics: 「發債當收入」, 「債冚債」).
- 2026-27 Budget (25 February 2026, 「創科驅動　金融賦能　多元發展　關愛惠民」): tax rebate capped at HK$3,000, rates waivers, extra social-security month (派糖), no consumption vouchers (消費券); higher stamp duty on homes above HK$100 million; 2% recurrent cuts; HK$150 billion transferred from the Exchange Fund to capital works for the Northern Metropolis (北都); bond issuance HK$160–220 billion a year; AI+ and 「金融+」; gold trading hub, stablecoin licences, Silver Bonds; HK$4 billion towards the Wang Fuk Court buyout. Appropriation Bill passed 29 April 2026 (his clash with Junius Ho 何君堯).
- Michael Wong leads the low-altitude economy (低空經濟) working group and the Wang Fuk Court rehousing and buyout.
- October 2026: GDP forecast 3.5–4.5%; APEC Finance Ministers' Meeting in Hong Kong (20–21 Oct); trips to Bangkok and Beijing; the 2027-28 Budget due early 2027. Named as a possible 2027 Chief Executive contender (「雙陳一鄧」).

## Easily confused
Chan Kin-por 陳健波 (another person); the Secretary for Financial Services and the Treasury (許正宇), who is below the FS; Chief Secretary Eric Chan 陳國基 (the other Chan); Under Secretaries vs the Deputy FS; the Budget (FS, February) vs the Policy Address (CE, September); fiscal reserves (HK$665.5 billion) vs the Exchange Fund (over HK$4.1 trillion); China's Minister of Finance (Lan Fo'an 藍佛安).
