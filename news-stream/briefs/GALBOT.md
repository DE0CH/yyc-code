# Galbot (銀河通用)

Status 22 October 2026.

## What it is
A Beijing embodied-AI (具身智能) company, registered 19 May 2023 in Haidian, that builds general-purpose robots and the models that control them. Products: Galbot G1 (wheeled two-armed robot, nicknamed 小蓋 / 蓋伯特), Galbot S1 (heavy-load industrial, January 2026), Galbot ET1 / 銀河星仔 (small bipedal humanoid, August 2026); robot-run mini shops 銀河太空艙 / Galbot Store (about 200 in nearly 50 mainland cities) and robot pharmacies (智慧藥房, with Meituan 美團買藥, 海王星辰, 老百姓大藥房). Models: 銀河星腦 / AstraBrain (WAM, WBC, Dex, Agent), GraspVLA, TrackVLA, NavFoM, LATENT, PASSAGE; a synthetic-data approach (vs teleoperation 遙操; "零遙操、全自主"). About RMB 7 billion raised by March 2026, valuation over USD 3 billion.

## Names
銀河通用 / 银河通用, 銀河通用機器人 / 機械人 (Hong Kong spelling), 北京銀河通用機器人股份有限公司, Galbot, Beijing Galbot Co., Galaxy General Robot. Subsidiaries: Shenzhen and Suzhou companies, 博銀合創 (50/50 joint venture with Bosch's 博原資本).

## People
Founder and CTO Wang He 王鶴 (Peking University EPIC Lab, BAAI); co-founder and GM Yao Tengzhou 姚騰洲 (legal representative); co-founder Zhang Zhizheng 張直政; chairman since July 2026 Yin Fangming 尹方鳴 (ROOBO founder, early Unitree investor; previous chairman 郭小亮); CFO Sun Dengke 孫登科; CSO Zhao Yuli 趙于莉; VP Chen Yang 陳旸.

## Investors and partners

Customers and partners: Bosch, Toyota, Hyundai, Zeekr, Great Wall, Siemens, NVIDIA (GTC 2026), Unitree (supplier), Peking University, BAAI, Suzhou High-tech Zone, Korean distributor TWIM; a RMB 236 million Yibin tender.

## Hong Kong
HKIC investment and talent-exchange centre (2024), office since 2024, Science Park. Galbot Store launched 31 August 2026 at Kai Tak Arts Centre (啟德體藝館) with Financial Secretary Paul Chan 陳茂波 (財爺) making the first purchase and HKIC CEO Clara Chan 陳家齊; stores at Kai Tak, Hung Hom waterfront (紅磡新海濱, Bauhinia Culture 紫荊文化), Wan Chai waterfront (灣仔海濱) and the Avenue of Stars (星光大道); press on slow service, Cantonese recognition and outages (「不如汽水機？」); plans for 10 or more stores. HKPC's embodied-AI hub partner (15 October 2026).
