# Chief Executive John Lee Ka-chiu (李家超) and the government he leads

Status 22 October 2026.

## What it is
The Chief Executive (行政長官, 特首, CE, "Hong Kong leader") heads the HKSAR (香港特別行政區). John Lee Ka-chiu (李家超), sixth-term CE, elected 8 May 2022 as sole candidate (1,416 votes), in office 1 July 2022 to 30 June 2027. He chairs the Committee for Safeguarding National Security (國安委), the Executive Council (行政會議, 行會, ExCo), the Council of Advisers (特首顧問團) and the Northern Metropolis committee. Posts on 港府 / 特區政府 / 第六屆政府 / the government as a whole often concern him.

## Names
李家超, John Lee, John KC Lee, LEE Ka Chiu, LI Jiachao; nicknames 超哥 (LIHKG), 比卡超, 頁問, 十一哥, 沙塵超. Office: 特首辦 / Chief Executive's Office (director Carol Yip 葉文娟), 禮賓府 (Government House), 添馬 / 政府總部 (Tamar). Policy Unit (特首政策組, Stephen Wong 黃元山).

## Background
Born 1957, ex-police (1977–2012, deputy commissioner), Under Secretary then Secretary for Security (2017–21, during the 2019 protests, 反修例), Chief Secretary 2021–22. US-sanctioned since 2020 (now under the Hong Kong Autonomy Act only, since July 2026).

## Current affairs
- First Five-Year Plan (《香港第一個五年規劃》, 2026–2030) and his fifth Policy Address (施政報告), 16 September 2026: "four centres, one hub" (四中心、一高地), Northern Metropolis (北都) university towns, housing, birth incentives, "AI City Brain" (AI城市大腦), civil-service accountability. LegCo passed both motions 9 October 2026.
- Wang Fuk Court fire (宏福苑大火, Tai Po, 26 Nov 2025, 168 dead): his accountability pledge, the Independent Committee (Justice David Lok) report handed to him 22 October 2026, reforms, the flat buy-out; critics' 「刑不上局長」.
- 2027 CE election (expected March 2027) and whether he seeks a second term (「10年魔咒」, 收風); speculated rivals 「雙陳一鄧」 (Eric Chan, Paul Chan, Chris Tang). Election Committee subsector polls 22 November 2026.
- Other: Article 23 law (2024), Jimmy Lai's conviction and 20-year sentence (2025–26), 2026-27 Budget, Tung Chee-hwa's death (Sept 2026), weekly pre-ExCo press remarks.

## Easily confused
李嘉誠 Li Ka-shing (tycoon, 「超人」); the Chief Secretary (政務司司長) is not the CE; 陳國基 vs 陳茂波 (「雙陳」); ExCo vs LegCo; Macau's CE Sam Hou Fai 岑浩輝 (also 「特首」); 國安委 (his committee) vs 國安公署 (Beijing's office); other people named John Lee.
