# Wang Fuk Court (宏福苑) and the Tai Po fire of 26 November 2025

Status 22 October 2026.

## What it is
Wang Fuk Court is a Home Ownership Scheme (居屋) court in Tai Po (大埔), New Territories: eight 31-storey blocks (1983, about 1,984 flats). On 26 November 2025, during a HK$330 million exterior renovation (大維修) with bamboo scaffolding (竹棚), non-fire-retardant netting (棚網 / 防護網) and foam boards (發泡膠板) over windows, a fire starting at Wang Cheong House (Block F, likely a cigarette 煙頭 in a light well) spread to seven blocks and burned over 43 hours. A No. 5 alarm (五級火). 168 dead (including firefighter Ho Wai-ho 何偉豪), 79 injured, about 4,600 residents homeless; the deadliest Hong Kong fire since 1948. The fire alarms did not sound (switches turned off), staircase windows had been removed.

## Names
宏福苑大火, 宏福苑五級火, 大埔五級火, 大埔火災, Tai Po fire / blaze, 宏福 (「宏福．前行」); "Wang Fu Court", "Hung Fuk Court". Blocks: 宏仁閣 A, 宏道閣 B, 宏新閣 C, 宏建閣 D, 宏泰閣 E, 宏昌閣 F, 宏盛閣 G, 宏志閣 Wang Chi House H (not burned). Neighbour Kwong Fuk Estate 廣福邨. Vocabulary: 法團 / 業主立案法團, 管委會, 授權票 (incl. 死人授權票), 圍標 (bid-rigging), 打龍通, 收購, 樓換樓, 特設銷售計劃, 揀樓, 一戶一社工, 災民, 街坊.

## Since the fire
- Arrests: 35 by police and ICAC. Charges 10 June 2026: manslaughter, conspiracy to defraud (tender fraud), money laundering, perverting justice; Buildings Department 259 charges, Labour Department 25. Next court mention 29 October 2026.
- Independent Committee (not a statutory Commission of Inquiry): 30 hearing days, 80 witnesses; report handed to John Lee 22 October 2026, reportedly blaming the ICU most, plus the Buildings, Fire Services and Labour departments, naming no officials; publication after legal advice.
- Rehousing: Blocks A–G to be demolished; buyout at HK$8,000 / HK$10,500 per sq ft (about HK$6.8 billion; buyer Wang Fuk Court Property Rights Acquisition Limited); Flat-for-Flat vouchers and a Special Sales Exercise in new HOS flats (e.g. 頌雅路西 Tai Po); Wang Chi House added; 99.3% accepted, 93.2% signed by 15 October; flat selection until late November.
- Reforms: construction-site smoking ban (17 July 2026); Policy Address amendments to the Fire Services, Buildings and Building Management Ordinances, criminalising bid-rigging, pre-qualified contractor lists; metal vs bamboo scaffolding.
- Debates: accountability of officials (「刑不上局長」), the inquiry format, ignored complaints before the fire, proxy votes and bid-rigging, buyout terms and insurance, speech arrests, the victims' names. First anniversary 26 November 2026.

## Easily confused
Kwong Fuk Estate 廣福邨 (next door, not burned); Cornwall Court 嘉禾大廈 (2008) and Garley Building 嘉利大廈 (1996) fires; Chinachem Tower and other 2025 scaffold fires; Grenfell Tower (2017); the 2018 Tai Po Road bus crash review; other fires in Tai Po.
