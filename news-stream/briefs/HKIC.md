# Hong Kong Investment Corporation (香港投資管理有限公司, 港投公司, HKIC)

Status 22 October 2026.

## What it is
A company wholly owned by the HKSAR government that invests fiscal-reserve money as "patient capital" (耐心資本) in companies and funds, with a dual mandate: medium-to-long-term return and Hong Kong's competitiveness. Starting capital HK$62 billion from four funds it manages: Hong Kong Growth Portfolio (香港增長組合, from the Future Fund 未來基金), Greater Bay Area Investment Fund (大灣區投資基金), Strategic Tech Fund (策略性創科基金) and the HK$30 billion Co-Investment Fund (共同投資基金). Announced in John Lee's 2022 Policy Address, board from February 2023, CEO from October 2023. It also runs the Investment Portfolio of the New Capital Investment Entrant Scheme (新資本投資者入境計劃, New CIES) through outside managers. Focus: hard and core tech, biotech and health tech, new energy and green tech; strategy 「投早投小」 (invest early and small). Office: One Pacific Place, Admiralty.

## Names
港投公司, 港投, 香港投資管理有限公司 / 香港投资管理有限公司, 香港投資公司, Hong Kong Investment Corp., HKIC, nickname 「港版淡馬錫」 ("Hong Kong's Temasek"). Slogans: 「耐心資本」, 「投資+」, 「1：8」/「一元撬動八元」 (co-investment leverage), 「港投生態圈」.

## Results and current affairs
- 2025 annual report (July 2026): investment income HK$6.459 billion, net IRR 14%, total assets about HK$69.7 billion. 250 investee projects (October 2026), 11 listed portfolio companies.
- Capital "largely allocated"; a further government injection is under discussion. An offshore renminbi venture capital fund (離岸人民幣創投基金) began investing in October 2026. Commercial property investment from the 2026-27 Budget. Named in the First Five-Year Plan and 2026 Policy Address (Northern Metropolis 北都, Nansha 南沙, Qianhai 前海, Hetao 河套).
- New CIES managers: 2024 and 2025 batches appointed; 2026 batch (at least HK$4 billion) opened 12 October 2026.
- Named investees and partners: SmartMore 思謀科技, Galbot 銀河通用 (robot shops at Kai Tak), StarFive 賽昉科技 (Lion Rock Chip 獅子山芯; Hong Kong RISC-V Alliance), StepFun 階躍星辰, Moonshot AI 月之暗面, OneRobotics 臥安機器人, Insilico Medicine 英矽智能, BioMap, Cornerstone Robotics 康諾思騰, WeLab, New Frontier Group 新風天域, Spark EV, EcoCeres 怡斯萊, Gobi Partners 戈壁創投, Lanchi 藍馳創投, Gaw Capital 基匯資本, BAAI 北京智源.
- Events: International Forum for Patient Capital (IPC), Start-up Investment and Development Summit, AI Global Talents Connect, Global Health Summit.
- Debates: transparency (no list of investments or amounts; LegCo questions), KPIs beyond return, share invested in Hong Kong vs the mainland, crowding out private VCs (與民爭利), the Temasek comparison, CEO pay.
