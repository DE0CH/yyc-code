# The Election Committee, its 2026 subsector elections and the 2027 Chief Executive election

Status 24 November 2026.

## What it is
The Election Committee (選舉委員會, 選委會, EC; members 選委) is Hong Kong's 1,500-member body that nominates and elects the Chief Executive (行政長官 / 特首) and elects 40 of LegCo's 90 members. Five sectors of 300 (Annex I of the Basic Law, rewritten in 2021 under 「完善選舉制度」 / 「愛國者治港」), 40 subsectors (界別分組): ex officio members (當然委員), members nominated by designated bodies (指定團體提名) and elected members (選任委員), mostly chosen by corporate voters. Five-year term. Candidates are vetted by the Candidate Eligibility Review Committee (資審會).

## The 2027 Chief Executive election
The seventh-term CE election (第七屆行政長官選舉), March 2027 (date not yet gazetted), budget HK$81 million. Steps: gazette notice, nomination period, vetting, one vote per member; a candidate needs at least 188 nominations with at least 15 from each sector, and more than 750 votes; the State Council appoints; the new term starts 1 July 2027, the 30th anniversary of the handover. John Lee 李家超 (elected unopposed in 2022 with 1,416 votes) has not said whether he will run; returning committee members mostly call support for his second term (連任) 「言之過早」 (Allan Zeman 盛智文 openly backs him). Beijing officials have been consulting sectors in Hong Kong on the next government and cabinet (收風, 北京密集調研); commentators expect hopefuls to declare from December; Richard Li 李澤楷 set out what he wants in the next CE; Regina Ip 葉劉淑儀 says she will not run. 「10年魔咒」: no CE has served two full terms. Vocabulary: 入閘, 參選, 跑出, 提名門檻, 拉票, 政綱, 中央欽點, 西環, 等額選舉, 一人模式, 陪跑, 黑馬, 程咬金.

## The 2026 Election Committee Subsector Ordinary Elections (選舉委員會界別分組一般選舉)
Polling 22 November 2026: 856 candidates for 723 seats in 31 contested subsectors, 259 elected unopposed, 341 ex officio and 156 nominated; 5,640 of 5,709 voters, turnout 98.79% (89.77% in 2021); counted electronically in 3 h 45 min; ties in Tourism and Architecture/surveying settled by lot. About 500 new faces; at least 482 returning members nominated Lee in 2022. Upsets: ex-treasury chief Ceajer Chan 陳家強 (Finance), Law Society president Roden Tong 湯文龍 (Legal), HKGCC chief executive 楊偉添 lost. Elected: Rimsky Yuen 袁國強, Sophia Chan 陳肇始, Alfred Sit 薛永恒, Eric Ma 馬紹祥, Libby Lee 李夏茵, Andrew Leung 梁君彥, Richard Li, Martin Lee Ka-shing 李家誠, Daryl Ng 黃永光, Justin Chiu 趙國雄, Benjamin Keswick; Exco members Jeffrey Lam 林健鋒, Chan Kin-por 陳健波, Ronny Tong 湯家驊, Margaret Leung 梁高美懿. Parties: DAB at least 91 seats, FTU 79, BPA 26, NPP 20, Liberal Party 10. The new (seventh) committee serves 1 February 2027 to 31 January 2032. Vocabulary: 自動當選, 差額選舉, 爆冷, 落馬, 墮馬, 祝福名單, 優中選優, 小圈子選舉, 優質民主, 良性競爭.

## People and bodies
Constitutional and Mainland Affairs Secretary Janice Tse 謝小華 (CMAB 政制及內地事務局); EAC (選管會) chairman Justice David Lok 陸啟康; Chief Electoral Officer Herman Tse 謝衍文 (REO 選舉事務處); HKMAO 港澳辦 (director Xia Baolong 夏寶龍) and the Liaison Office 中聯辦; commentators Lau Siu-kai 劉兆佳, Sonny Lo 盧兆興. Also due: the District Council election by end-2027.

## Easily confused
The Election Committee constituency of LegCo (選委界, 40 LegCo seats, last elected December 2025); functional constituencies (功能界別); EC by-elections (補選); the EAC (runs elections) vs the EC; the November 2026 vote (chooses electors) vs the March 2027 vote (chooses the CE); the CE's day-to-day work and policies (the CEHK stream's subject) vs the race for the post; the principal officials' appointments after the election; the District Council election; Macau's CE election; Taiwan's Central Election Commission (中央選舉委員會).
