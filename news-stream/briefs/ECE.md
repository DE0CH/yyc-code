# The Election Committee, its 2026 subsector elections and the 2027 Chief Executive election

Status 22 October 2026.

## What it is
The Election Committee (選舉委員會, 選委會, EC; members 選委) is Hong Kong's 1,500-member body that nominates and elects the Chief Executive (行政長官 / 特首) and elects 40 of LegCo's 90 members. Five sectors of 300 (Annex I of the Basic Law, rewritten in 2021 under 「完善選舉制度」 / 「愛國者治港」): industrial, commercial and financial; the professions; grassroots, labour and religious; LegCo members and district organisations; NPC deputies, CPPCC members and national organisations (「國家隊」). 40 subsectors (界別分組). Seats: 362 ex officio, 156 nominated by designated bodies, 982 elected by mostly corporate voters (9,342 on the register). Five-year term. Candidates are vetted by the Candidate Eligibility Review Committee (資審會) on national-security advice.

## The 2026 Election Committee Subsector Ordinary Elections (選舉委員會界別分組一般選舉)
- Polling Sunday 22 November 2026, 9 a.m.–6 p.m., electronic counting at the Convention and Exhibition Centre; budget HK$260 million. Nominations 7–20 October: 1,115 candidates for 982 seats; 31 of 36 subsectors contested (13 in 2021), five uncontested (Education, Sports/performing arts/culture/publication, fellow townsmen 同鄉社團, Labour, national organisations). Fiercest: Social welfare (25 for 15), Medical and health (23 for 15); all 18 First Sector subsectors contested; Heung Yee Kuk 鄉議局 and Agriculture and fisheries contested for the first time. 5,757 voters in contested subsectors.
- Notable candidates: Rimsky Yuen 袁國強, Sophia Chan 陳肇始, Libby Lee 李夏茵, Alfred Sit 薛永恒, Eric Ma 馬紹祥, Ceajer Chan 陳家強, Andrew Leung 梁君彥, Michael Luk 陸頌雄, Aron Kwok 郭偉强 (FTU 工聯會 runs 46 for 60 Labour seats), Martin Lee Ka-shing 李家誠, Justin Chiu 趙國雄, Richard Li 李澤楷, Daryl Ng 黃永光, Benjamin Keswick, Derek Yee 爾冬陞 (team 創動領航 backed by Kenneth Fok 霍啟剛). Not running: Tik Chi-yuen 狄志遠, Gordon Wu 胡應湘, Wong Pik-kiu 黃碧嬌.
- Next: vetting and gazettal, candidates' briefing 3 November, the new (seventh) Election Committee from 1 February 2027 to 31 January 2032. Previous rounds: 2021 (89.77% turnout, mostly uncontested), by-election 7 September 2025.
- Vocabulary: 自動當選, 等額 / 超額 / 差額選舉, 自由搏擊, 溫提, 友好溝通, 祝福名單, 八十大限, 大執位, 老奉, 冧莊, 跑馬仔, 鐵票, 小圈子選舉, 優質民主, 良性競爭, 清一色, 無得揀, 爭崩頭, 一人模式.

## The 2027 Chief Executive election
March 2027 (date not yet gazetted), budget HK$81 million. A candidate needs at least 188 nominations with at least 15 from each sector, and more than 750 votes; the State Council appoints. John Lee 李家超 (term ends 30 June 2027; elected unopposed in 2022 with 1,416 votes) has not said whether he will run (「心無雜念」); Beijing reportedly gathering views (收風); 「10年魔咒」: no CE has served two full terms. Regina Ip 葉劉淑儀 says she will not run. Also due: District Council election by end-2027.

## People and bodies

## Easily confused
The Election Committee constituency of LegCo (選委界, 40 LegCo seats, last elected December 2025); functional constituencies (功能界別); EC by-elections (補選); the EAC (runs elections) vs the EC; the November 2026 vote (chooses electors) vs the March 2027 vote (chooses the CE); the District Council election; the 1996 Selection Committee (推選委員會); Macau's CE election committee; Taiwan's Central Election Commission (中央選舉委員會).
