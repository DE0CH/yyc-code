# Hong Kong political gossip and insider political reporting (政治花絮 / 政壇專欄)

Status 22 October 2026.

## What it is
A recurring genre of Hong Kong political columns about what is said in private: who is rising or falling, who has Beijing's backing, who will be reappointed or dropped, what was said at closed meetings or dinners, how legislators behave. Usually under a pen name or column title, citing 政界人士, 建制中人, 知情人士. Examples: Sing Tao 《大棋盤》 (聶風), East Week 《政官莊》, HK01 《一片破局》 / 《拆局》 (政情), HKET 《有政戲》 (傅流螢), am730 "Ray Online", Oriental Daily 《撥亂反正》 (黃傑士), etnet 【香港政情】, Sing Tao's overseas editions.

## Vocabulary
政情, 拆局, 收風 / 收風人 (Beijing gathering opinions), 阿爺 / 中央 (Beijing), 西環 (the Liaison Office), 述職 (the CE's duty report), 上京, 冧莊 (incumbent stays), 換馬, 埋班, 君子之爭 / 君子賽, 揭盅, 自動當選, 差額選舉, 新丁, 翻生, 7字頭議員 (legislators over 70), 大主席 (LegCo President), 班長 (camp convenor), 武官治港 (disciplined-services officials in top posts), 祝福, 掉隊, 零發言. Nicknames: 超哥 (John Lee), 強哥 / 蛋強 (Chris Tang).

## Beijing's bodies
港澳辦 / 中央港澳辦 (HKMAO, Xia Baolong 夏寶龍; deputies Xu Qifang 徐啟方, Zhou Ji 周霽, Zheng Xincong 鄭新聰, Nong Rong 農融, Zhang Dongmei 章冬梅); the Central Leading Group (Ding Xuexiang 丁薛祥); 中聯辦 (Zhou Ji since May 2025, replacing Zheng Yanxiong 鄭雁雄; deputy Yuan Gujie 袁古潔; restructured July 2026); 國安公署 (Dong Jingwei 董經緯); the Foreign Ministry commissioner (Cui Jianchun 崔建春); the PLA garrison.

## Easily confused
港澳辦 vs 中聯辦; 鄭雁雄 vs 鄭新聰; 陳茂波 vs 陳振英 vs 陳克勤 vs 陳健波; 吳秋北 vs 吳永嘉; Eric Chan vs Erick Tsang; 大主席 = LegCo President; the November 2026 Election Committee poll vs the March 2027 CE election; 中聯辦 vs 國安公署.
