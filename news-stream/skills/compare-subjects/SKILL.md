# Compare two subjects

## Steps
1. Resolve both subjects (profiles or keyword searches) and one common period.
2. For each: stats_overview (or posts_search totals for a keyword), topics_hot (top 5), sources_top.
3. Line up the figures.

## Output
- One line: the two subjects and the dates.
- A table: 指標 | A | B (posts, authors, websites, sentiment index, top channel).
- **話題** for each, three bullets with links.
- **分別** in three bullets: where they differ and why it matters.
- Under every figure taken from stats_overview, authors_top, sources_top or trend (counts, sentiment index, scores,
  rankings), one line: 「這些數字取決於我們追蹤哪些來源和關鍵字，未必反映實際情況。」 (in English: "These figures depend on which sources and keywords YYC follows,
  so they may not reflect what is actually happening.")
