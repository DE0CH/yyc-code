1Summary

  • Official route (Meta's Cloud API): every alert to a Hong Kong number outside a 24-hour window the reader opened is a paid template message: USD 0.026 as a utility template, USD 0.0732 as a marketing template ⟦meta-rates⟧ ⟦meta-pricing⟧. Since 1 October 2026 the free messages inside a reader-opened window are also charged after 1,000 per number per month ⟦meta-pricing⟧.
  • Cost at YYC's measured volume (486 filed items on 15 Oct across the 8 streams with a Telegram channel ⟦yyc-volume⟧), one WhatsApp message per item as on Telegram: USD 3,790.80/month for 10 readers of every stream and USD 18,201.30/month for 50, at Meta's utility rate (Claude's calculation from Meta's rates; section 5).
  • Official groups (Groups API) hold at most 8 people, need an Official Business Account (blue tick), and bill each delivered member as one message ⟦meta-groups⟧ ⟦meta-groups-pricing⟧. They cost the same as one-to-one messages.
  • WhatsApp Channels (the Telegram-channel analogue) have no posting endpoint in Meta's Cloud API ⟦wapito⟧, and every channel is public: "It's not possible to create a private channel." ⟦faq-ch-admin-privacy⟧
  • Unofficial route (a linked WhatsApp account driven by Baileys, whatsmeow, whatsapp-web.js or a hosted wrapper) posts into ordinary groups of up to 1,024 members ⟦faq-groupsize⟧ for a flat USD 0 to 40/month ⟦whapi⟧ ⟦maytapi⟧ ⟦greenapi⟧, with no per-message charge. WhatsApp's Terms forbid automated messaging and non-personal use ⟦wa-tos⟧, and its Help Center says linked unofficial clients can be temporarily or permanently banned ⟦faq-unofficial⟧.
  • K-Matrix today posts its alerts into three ordinary WhatsApp groups from a plain +852 mobile number, all 201 archived alerts from that one sender ⟦yyc-kmatrix⟧.

Decisions for Deyao

  1. Official or unofficial. Official: Meta's per-message charges (USD 473.85 to 18,201.30 a month in the scenarios below), a YYC Meta business portfolio, business verification with YYC's documents, opt-in records. Unofficial: a flat fee or nothing, ordinary groups like K-Matrix's, but against WhatsApp's Terms with a ban risk on the sending number.
  2. Delivery shape. One group per stream (readers join by invite link; works on both routes, up to 8 people per group on the official route) or per-reader subscriptions (a reader messages the YYC number and picks streams; official route only in practice).
  3. One message per item, or batches. Telegram sends one message per item. On the official route cost scales with message count; sending one message per stream per half hour cuts 486 messages a day to 159 (15 Oct) (Claude's count; section 5).
  4. The sending number. A new number used only for this: a Hong Kong SIM must be real-name registered ⟦ofca⟧; on the official route the number stops working in the normal WhatsApp app ⟦meta-phone⟧.
  5. Who receives it. Staff only, or clients too. Clients raise the opt-in requirement ⟦meta-optin⟧ and, on the unofficial route, put a ban on YYC's client-facing number in front of clients (Claude's inference).

4Meta's rules that decide the official route

The 24-hour window and templates

  • Non-template messages "can only be sent in an open 24-hour customer service window, which opens and resets with each user message"; template messages can be delivered at any time ⟦meta-pricing⟧.
  • Meta charges per delivered message, by category and by the recipient's country code ⟦meta-pricing⟧.
  • From 1 October 2026: "Meta provides every business phone number with a free tier of 1,000 service messages per month"; charges start at the 1,001st ⟦meta-pricing⟧. Before that date service messages and utility templates inside the window were free ⟦udhk⟧.
  • Hong Kong became a standalone market on 1 July 2026; its utility and authentication rate went from USD 0.0113 to USD 0.026 ⟦whatools-q3⟧.
  • Free entry point: when a user messages from a Click-to-WhatsApp ad or a Facebook Page button, all messages in that window are free; "The FEP window may remain open for up to 7 days." ⟦meta-pricing⟧ (Meta's marketing page still says 72 hours ⟦meta-rates⟧.)

Is a news alert utility or marketing?

  • Utility must be "non-promotional" and "either specific to or requested by the user ... OR essential or critical to the user" ⟦meta-categ⟧.
  • Meta's utility example "Account Alerts or Updates": "important or time-sensitive updates or alerts or other information specific to purchased or subscribed products/services" ⟦meta-categ⟧.
  • Meta's marketing example "Awareness": "Generate awareness of your business, products, or services among customers who have subscribed" ⟦meta-categ⟧.
  • Since 9 April 2025 a template submitted as utility that Meta judges to be marketing is approved as marketing ⟦meta-categ⟧.
  • Claude's inference: a monitoring alert a reader subscribed to fits "alerts ... specific to subscribed services"; Meta decides at review, so the marketing rate is the upper bound.

Account, number and verification

  • A Cloud API number "cannot be used with WhatsApp Messenger"; a number already on WhatsApp must be deleted there first; it must have a country and area code and receive SMS or voice calls; landlines are listed but marked "Not Recommended" ⟦meta-phone⟧.
  • New portfolios can register 2 numbers; 20 after verification ⟦meta-phone⟧.
  • Business verification uploads official documents proving the business is registered; the accepted list is per country ⟦360-bv⟧.
  • Without a payment method, Meta stops delivering service messages after the free tier ⟦meta-pricing⟧.
  • Opt-in must name the business and say what the person is opting in to; website, SMS, phone and paper are accepted methods ⟦meta-optin⟧.
  • Business Messaging Policy: "Organizations that primarily publish news content must be registered as a News Page through Facebook." ⟦wa-policy⟧ Claude's inference: YYC is a PR consultancy, not primarily a news publisher, but its alerts are news content.

Format and receipts

  • Text messages carry a body and an optional link preview (preview_url) ⟦meta-text⟧. Telegram sends with previews off ⟦yyc-telegram⟧.
  • Status webhooks report sent, delivered and read, with pricing data on delivered or read ⟦meta-groups-pricing⟧.

6Delivery shape and the unofficial route's ban risk

Group per stream vs per-reader subscription

  • Group per stream (the Telegram shape): official route via the Groups API, 8 people per group, each member billed ⟦meta-groups⟧ ⟦meta-groups-pricing⟧; unofficial route via ordinary groups of up to 1,024 ⟦faq-groupsize⟧, one message per item whatever the group size (Claude's inference from how group messages work). An announcement-only community group lets only admins post ⟦faq-community⟧.
  • Per-reader subscription: a reader messages YYC's number (for example "訂閱 CEHK"); that opens a 24-hour window ⟦meta-pricing⟧ and is a record of opt-in ⟦meta-optin⟧. Every alert after the window still needs a template ⟦meta-pricing⟧. On the unofficial route, messaging people one by one is where the reported bans happen: Baileys error 463 "Reachout Timelock" on "users who haven't been contacted before" ⟦gh-2707⟧.

Ban risk on the unofficial route: the evidence

  • WhatsApp: linking an account to unofficial versions "violates our Terms of Service"; the account "might also be temporarily or permanently banned, or ... restrictions on your account, including the ability to link devices" ⟦faq-unofficial⟧.
  • WhatsApp: "Our products are not intended for bulk or automated messaging"; it takes "legal action against individuals or companies" linked to such abuse ⟦faq-bulk⟧.
  • whatsapp-web.js: "it is not guaranteed you will not be blocked ... WhatsApp does not allow bots or unofficial clients" ⟦wwebjs⟧. Baileys: "We discourage any stalkerware, bulk or automated messaging usage" ⟦baileys⟧.
  • Reports: a ban after a loop of sendMessage to many recipients (Baileys #2850, 2026-10-07) ⟦gh-2850⟧; a 24-hour ban after about 3 messages to new contacts despite 20–35 s random delays (whatsapp-web.js #4008) ⟦gh-4008⟧; an instant restriction when a blue-tick OBA number was linked to Baileys (Baileys #2805) ⟦gh-2805⟧.
  • Claude's inference: every report found concerns messaging people one by one or bulk lists; none concerns posting into the account's own groups that members joined by link, which is K-Matrix's pattern ⟦yyc-kmatrix⟧. That lowers the risk; it does not remove it, and a ban takes the number and its groups with it.
  • A Hong Kong SIM for the number must be real-name registered before activation ⟦ofca⟧.
