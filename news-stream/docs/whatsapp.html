WhatsApp delivery for YYC's streams: every route, with costs

Telegram carries every filed item today. This compares the ways to send the same alerts on WhatsApp, at YYC's measured volume. Prepared by Claude for Deyao; investigation only, nothing built or bought.

1Summary

  • Official route (Meta's Cloud API): every alert to a Hong Kong number outside a 24-hour window the reader opened is a paid template message: USD 0.026 as a utility template, USD 0.0732 as a marketing template1,2. Since 1 October 2026 the free messages inside a reader-opened window are also charged after 1,000 per number per month2.
  • Cost at YYC's measured volume (486 filed items on 15 Oct across the 8 streams with a Telegram channel3), one WhatsApp message per item as on Telegram: USD 3,790.80/month for 10 readers of every stream and USD 18,201.30/month for 50, at Meta's utility rate (Claude's calculation from Meta's rates; section 5).
  • Official groups (Groups API) hold at most 8 people, need an Official Business Account (blue tick), and bill each delivered member as one message4,5. They cost the same as one-to-one messages.
  • WhatsApp Channels (the Telegram-channel analogue) have no posting endpoint in Meta's Cloud API6, and every channel is public: "It's not possible to create a private channel."7
  • Unofficial route (a linked WhatsApp account driven by Baileys, whatsmeow, whatsapp-web.js or a hosted wrapper) posts into ordinary groups of up to 1,024 members8 for a flat USD 0 to 40/month9,10,11, with no per-message charge. WhatsApp's Terms forbid automated messaging and non-personal use12, and its Help Center says linked unofficial clients can be temporarily or permanently banned13.
  • K-Matrix today posts its alerts into three ordinary WhatsApp groups from a plain +852 mobile number, all 201 archived alerts from that one sender14.

Decisions for Deyao

  1. Official or unofficial. Official: Meta's per-message charges (USD 473.85 to 18,201.30 a month in the scenarios below), a YYC Meta business portfolio, business verification with YYC's documents, opt-in records. Unofficial: a flat fee or nothing, ordinary groups like K-Matrix's, but against WhatsApp's Terms with a ban risk on the sending number.
  2. Delivery shape. One group per stream (readers join by invite link; works on both routes, up to 8 people per group on the official route) or per-reader subscriptions (a reader messages the YYC number and picks streams; official route only in practice).
  3. One message per item, or batches. Telegram sends one message per item. On the official route cost scales with message count; sending one message per stream per half hour cuts 486 messages a day to 159 (15 Oct) (Claude's count; section 5).
  4. The sending number. A new number used only for this: a Hong Kong SIM must be real-name registered15; on the official route the number stops working in the normal WhatsApp app16.
  5. Who receives it. Staff only, or clients too. Clients raise the opt-in requirement17 and, on the unofficial route, put a ban on YYC's client-facing number in front of clients (Claude's inference).

3Every route, side by side

A. Meta Cloud API, one-to-one

official
Sends to
Individual WhatsApp users. A business may start a conversation only with a template message; free-form messages are allowed only inside a 24-hour window opened by the user's own message2.
Price (HK numbers)
Marketing USD 0.0732, utility USD 0.026, authentication USD 0.026, service USD 0.026 per delivered message; utility drops to USD 0.0247 above 150,000 a month1. Service messages: first 1,000 per business number per month free2. No platform fee.
YYC provides
A Meta business portfolio and developer app19, a Messaging account with a payment method20, a phone number able to receive an SMS or voice call that is not in use on WhatsApp16, opt-in from each reader17. Business verification raises the limits21.
Limits
250 unique users per 24 hours outside windows for a new portfolio; 2,000 after business verification21. Enough for 50 readers.
Risks
Meta may re-categorise a utility template as marketing (2.8 times the price)22. Quality rating: blocks and reports can rate-limit the number17.
Expense isolation
A Messaging account holds YYC's templates and billing details20; a YYC-only portfolio with its own payment method makes Meta's billing records YYC's alone (Claude's inference).

B. Meta Cloud API, Groups API

official
Sends to
Groups the business creates. Readers join through an invite link. At most 8 participants per group, up to 10,000 groups per business number, text and media and templates; interactive messages, edits and deletes are not supported4.
Eligibility
Official Business Account only4. OBA needs business verification, 30 days on the platform, two-step verification and an approved display name23. Not available on numbers shared with the WhatsApp Business app (Coexistence)4.
Price
Charged per delivered member at that member's rate: a template to a group of 5 is 5 charges5. Any member's message opens a window for the whole group; since 1 October 2026 utility templates inside it are charged and non-template messages are free only within the number's 1,000-a-month free tier, one unit per delivered member5.
Fit
Same cost as route A per reader. 10 readers of one stream need 2 groups (Claude's arithmetic from the 8-person cap).
Expense isolation
As route A.

4Meta's rules that decide the official route

The 24-hour window and templates

  • Non-template messages "can only be sent in an open 24-hour customer service window, which opens and resets with each user message"; template messages can be delivered at any time2.
  • Meta charges per delivered message, by category and by the recipient's country code2.
  • From 1 October 2026: "Meta provides every business phone number with a free tier of 1,000 service messages per month"; charges start at the 1,001st2. Before that date service messages and utility templates inside the window were free49.
  • Hong Kong became a standalone market on 1 July 2026; its utility and authentication rate went from USD 0.0113 to USD 0.02650.
  • Free entry point: when a user messages from a Click-to-WhatsApp ad or a Facebook Page button, all messages in that window are free; "The FEP window may remain open for up to 7 days."2 (Meta's marketing page still says 72 hours1.)

Is a news alert utility or marketing?

  • Utility must be "non-promotional" and "either specific to or requested by the user ... OR essential or critical to the user"22.
  • Meta's utility example "Account Alerts or Updates": "important or time-sensitive updates or alerts or other information specific to purchased or subscribed products/services"22.
  • Meta's marketing example "Awareness": "Generate awareness of your business, products, or services among customers who have subscribed"22.
  • Since 9 April 2025 a template submitted as utility that Meta judges to be marketing is approved as marketing22.
  • Claude's inference: a monitoring alert a reader subscribed to fits "alerts ... specific to subscribed services"; Meta decides at review, so the marketing rate is the upper bound.

Account, number and verification

  • A Cloud API number "cannot be used with WhatsApp Messenger"; a number already on WhatsApp must be deleted there first; it must have a country and area code and receive SMS or voice calls; landlines are listed but marked "Not Recommended"16.
  • New portfolios can register 2 numbers; 20 after verification16.
  • Business verification uploads official documents proving the business is registered; the accepted list is per country51.
  • Without a payment method, Meta stops delivering service messages after the free tier2.
  • Opt-in must name the business and say what the person is opting in to; website, SMS, phone and paper are accepted methods17.
  • Business Messaging Policy: "Organizations that primarily publish news content must be registered as a News Page through Facebook."52 Claude's inference: YYC is a PR consultancy, not primarily a news publisher, but its alerts are news content.

Format and receipts

  • Text messages carry a body and an optional link preview (preview_url)53. Telegram sends with previews off18.
  • Status webhooks report sent, delivered and read, with pricing data on delivered or read5.

6Delivery shape and the unofficial route's ban risk

Group per stream vs per-reader subscription

  • Group per stream (the Telegram shape): official route via the Groups API, 8 people per group, each member billed4,5; unofficial route via ordinary groups of up to 1,0248, one message per item whatever the group size (Claude's inference from how group messages work). An announcement-only community group lets only admins post44.
  • Per-reader subscription: a reader messages YYC's number (for example "訂閱 CEHK"); that opens a 24-hour window2 and is a record of opt-in17. Every alert after the window still needs a template2. On the unofficial route, messaging people one by one is where the reported bans happen: Baileys error 463 "Reachout Timelock" on "users who haven't been contacted before"54.

Message format (Claude's draft, Traditional Chinese)

【CEHK】🔴👀 {標題}

The same fields as Telegram18. On the official route this is a template with variables, submitted for approval22.

Ban risk on the unofficial route: the evidence

  • WhatsApp: linking an account to unofficial versions "violates our Terms of Service"; the account "might also be temporarily or permanently banned, or ... restrictions on your account, including the ability to link devices"13.
  • WhatsApp: "Our products are not intended for bulk or automated messaging"; it takes "legal action against individuals or companies" linked to such abuse45.
  • whatsapp-web.js: "it is not guaranteed you will not be blocked ... WhatsApp does not allow bots or unofficial clients"38. Baileys: "We discourage any stalkerware, bulk or automated messaging usage"39.
  • Reports: a ban after a loop of sendMessage to many recipients (Baileys #2850, 2026-10-07)55; a 24-hour ban after about 3 messages to new contacts despite 20–35 s random delays (whatsapp-web.js #4008)56; an instant restriction when a blue-tick OBA number was linked to Baileys (Baileys #2805)57.
  • Claude's inference: every report found concerns messaging people one by one or bulk lists; none concerns posting into the account's own groups that members joined by link, which is K-Matrix's pattern14. That lowers the risk; it does not remove it, and a ban takes the number and its groups with it.
  • A Hong Kong SIM for the number must be real-name registered before activation15.

7Expense evidence for the YYC claim

  • Meta direct: a Meta business portfolio and Messaging account used only for YYC, Deyao's card as its payment method; Meta's billing for that account is the evidence (a Messaging account "holds your message templates and billing details"20).
  • BSP: a YYC-only BSP account; the BSP's invoice (Twilio, Bird and others bill Meta's fee and their own together24,25).
  • Hosted unofficial: a YYC-only subscription; the provider's invoice.
  • Self-hosted: Hetzner project yyc (already on the list) plus the SIM's receipt.
  • Whichever is chosen gets its line in EXPENSES.md (YYC rule).
